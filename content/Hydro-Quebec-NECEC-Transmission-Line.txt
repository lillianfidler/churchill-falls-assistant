1. WHAT IS THE NEW ENGLAND CLEAN ENERGY CONNECT?

The New England Clean Energy Connect (NECEC) is a high-voltage electricity transmission corridor intended to transport hydroelectric energy from Quebec into the New England electricity market.

The project has a transmission capacity of approximately 1,200 megawatts (MW) and can supply electricity to roughly 1 million homes.

The project includes:
- A transmission line from the Quebec-Maine border into Maine
- High-Voltage Direct Current (HVDC) transmission infrastructure
- A converter station in Lewiston, Maine
- Upgrades to existing Alternating Current (AC) transmission systems in Maine
- Integration into the broader New England power grid

The electricity delivered through the system primarily originates from Hydro-Quebec's hydroelectric generating facilities.
